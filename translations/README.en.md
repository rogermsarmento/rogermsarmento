Professor & Researcher based in Fortaleza, Ceará, Brazil.  
Holding a **Ph.D. in Teleinformatics Engineering** from the Federal University of Ceará (UFC, 2021) and an **M.Sc. in Computer Science** from the Federal Institute of Ceará (IFCE, 2016).

Currently serving as an Associate Professor at IFCE and a researcher at the **Laboratory of Image Processing and Computer Simulation (LAPISCO)** within the Graduate Program in Computer Science (PPGCC).

### 🔬 Research & Focus Areas

* **Fields:** Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Digital Image Processing (DIP), and Computer Vision.
